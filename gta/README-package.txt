BeamNG x Los Santos
===================

The real BeamNG.drive car in GTA V Legacy's Los Santos (story mode only).
Installed and started by Melty: BeamNG starts first, then GTA V.

In GTA V story mode
  F6   choose a BeamNG car (BeamNG's vehicle selector opens; press F6 in BeamNG to come back)
  F7   BeamNG's tuning menu for your car
  F8   switch the mashup off / on
  E    repair at a Los Santos Customs door (stop the car in front of it)

Story mode only. BeamLS switches itself off for good if GTA Online starts.
Files it adds to the GTA V folder: BeamLS.asi, ReShade64.asi, ReShade.ini, BeamLS\ (preset, shader, log).
Files it adds to BeamNG: the mod folder mods\unpacked\beamls in your BeamNG user folder.
Log: BeamLS\BeamLS.log in the GTA V folder.
